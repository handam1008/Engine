## Squirrel — Enemy_Squirrel_ScrewThief_Concept_v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ONE original ENEMY UNIT concept sprite for a 2D side-scrolling game inside an old Windows XP computer. The enemy belongs to a mischievous gang of computer-part thieves.
STYLE: Very simple, bold, deliberately economical chunky pixel cartoon. Silhouette first. Thick solid dark-slate stepped contour, compact dot eyes, minimal facial marks, large uninterrupted flat areas of color, no more than one flat shadow shade per material. Draw like a small 64-pixel-tall game enemy enlarged with nearest-neighbor, with consistent chunky pixels. Side view with slight three-quarter turn toward SCREEN LEFT so the face reads. Comical, mildly troublesome, caught-in-the-act expression. Simple believable animal anatomy with exaggerated proportions; no clothes or equipment.
OUTPUT: A single full-body ENEMY UNIT, one pose, centered with at least 12% transparent padding on all sides, genuinely transparent alpha background. No ground, cast shadow, scenery, other creatures, comparison sheet, labels, UI, text, panels, or effects.
AVOID: generic slime or virus blobs, glitch holes, floating blocks, huge sparkling anime eyes, eye reflections, blush, human anatomy, armor, decorative accessories, glossy 3D volume, smooth gradients, furry texture, noisy pixel details, airbrush, anti-aliasing, rim lighting, realistic anatomy, a product mascot posing frontally. These are enemies, not allied XP tools or app icons. Do not copy a specific existing game's creature design.

SUBJECT: A little SQUIRREL COMPUTER-PART THIEF. Clearly identifiable squirrel with one enormous high arched bushy tail rising behind its compact hunched body, two small rounded ears, a short snout and compact dark dot eyes. Warm subdued chestnut body, pale cream muzzle and belly, a single broad deeper brown shadow plane. Entire tail is one simple connected bold curved silhouette, no individual hair marks.
ACTION AND PROP: Facing screen LEFT, crouched low and hunched protectively over ONE stolen oversized SILVER COMPUTER SCREW, clutched tightly in its two front paws against its belly. The screw is roughly half the squirrel's torso height: a readable broad screw head and only three coarse thread notches, very few shapes. Rear paws planted as if about to scuttle away. Head glances toward the left with a tiny guilty flat mouth. The screw is being hoarded, not brandished as a weapon. Keep the tail, screw and body all visible and uncropped.
