## Squirrel — Enemy_Squirrel_Basic_v3.png

Use the attached cat image ONLY as the visual style guide for a NEW ENEMY UNIT. Match its very simple coarse PIXEL ART, thick dark-slate square-stepped outline, flat white unshaded body, tiny square-dot eyes, compact face, and short block feet. The level of detail must be as low as this reference. Use about 32 logical pixels in the full creature height, enlarged by nearest neighbor: big consistent square pixels, never smooth/vector lines. Two opaque colors only: white #F8F8F8 and dark slate #252B30. ONE basic enemy in ONE neutral idle pose facing slightly LEFT, fully visible with generous clear padding. Genuine transparent background; the black surround in the reference is NOT part of the asset. No cat in the output. No objects, equipment, stolen parts, lettering, scenery, shadows, gradients, grain, texture, glow, soft edges, fur, decorative details, big expressive eyes or lighting. This is enemy art only.
Draw a SQUIRREL ENEMY: a squat chubby white pear body, two tiny rounded square-stepped ears, ONE large simply C-curled tail behind on the right, two tiny stub feet. Body height roughly equal to its width. Two tiny square-dot eyes and a tiny two-pixel flat mouth on the upper-left front of the body. No separate head-neck joint. No visible hands or forearms, no belly patch, cheeks, fingers, toes, fur marks or detailed snout. The tail has only its outer outline and at most one short dark inner curl. A silly blank expression. Large simple connected white shapes, detail budget matching the reference cat. It should look like a very basic enemy from the same set.
